# GitView Voice & Copy

## Voice
Clear, Calm, Precise, Trustworthy. Write for developers who want the product to get out of the way.

## Primary line
**All of Git, one window.**

## Descriptor
Commit, history, blame, branches, stash, rebase, PR review — plus 3-way merge from real Git stages.

## Positioning rule (do not regress)
GitView is the **full Git tool window**, used every day. Merge conflict resolution is one of
eight capabilities, not the headline. Never describe GitView as a merge tool, a conflict
resolver, or "3-way merge for VS Code" — that framing hands the category to the built-in
merge editor and to single-purpose extensions, and throws away seven eighths of the product.
Lead with the daily loop (commit · history · blame · branch · stash · rebase · review), then
say "and when a merge breaks".

## UI copy rules
- Sentence case: `Add account`, not `Add Account`.
- Prefer verbs: `Format`, `Switch`, `Open`, `Resolve`.
- Be concrete; avoid hype such as “revolutionary”, “magical”, or “AI-powered” when the capability can be named directly.
- Keep errors actionable: state what happened and the next available action.
- Do not end standalone taglines with extra punctuation beyond the approved wording.
