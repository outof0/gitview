# GitView Voice & Copy

## Voice
Clear, Calm, Precise, Trustworthy. Write for developers who want the product to get out of the way.

## Primary line
**See Git clearly.**

## Descriptor
History, branches, changes and diffs — finally in view.

## UI copy rules
- Sentence case: `Add account`, not `Add Account`.
- Prefer verbs: `Format`, `Switch`, `Open`, `Resolve`.
- Be concrete; avoid hype such as “revolutionary”, “magical”, or “AI-powered” when the capability can be named directly.
- Keep errors actionable: state what happened and the next available action.
- Do not end standalone taglines with extra punctuation beyond the approved wording.
